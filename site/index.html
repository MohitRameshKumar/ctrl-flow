Your browser, on repeat.

Cadence notices the routines you repeat — the same three tabs every morning, the same check before standup — and gives you one key that runs them.

Download

Getting started

1

Open the file you downloaded and install Cadence.

2

Launch it. Cadence opens a browser window of its own and starts watching. You will sign in to your sites once — that profile is then remembered.

3

Browse normally. When you repeat something, press Ctrl Shift Y then R and Cadence offers to keep it as a macro.

Before it opens the first time

Cadence is not code-signed yet, so your operating system will warn you the first time. That warning is about the absence of a paid certificate, not about anything the app does.

What it records

Everything stays on your machine. Pages, clicks and time spent are stored in a local database. Nothing is uploaded unless you ask for a standup draft.

It never records what you type. Only that typing happened, and in which field — never the keystrokes themselves.

Sensitive sites are excluded before you start. Banks, email and password managers are on the blocklist by default; you can add your own.

You can wipe it. History retention is a setting, from ten minutes to seven days.

Cadence · requires Google Chrome, Edge or Brave · Windows and macOS
